# Remix of Remix of SDA Finance Hub

Design a modern, secure, and responsive web-based Church Treasurer Cash Flow and Offering Management System for the Seventh-day Adventist Church. The system should automate the manual Church Treasurer's Cash Statement and Cash Count & Offerings Report Summary forms by replacing paper-based bookkeeping with a digital platform that performs automatic calculations, generates reports, tracks church income and expenses, and simplifies conference remittance. The application should have a clean, professional, and user-friendly interface that can be easily used by church treasurers with minimal computer skills. It should work seamlessly on desktop computers, tablets, and mobile devices while using a modern color theme inspired by the Seventh-day Adventist Church, incorporating dark green, white, light gray, and subtle gold accents.

The system should support two main user roles: Administrator and Church Treasurer. The Administrator should have full control over the system, including creating churches and districts, managing users and their roles, configuring offering categories, managing financial years, backing up and restoring data, and viewing reports from all churches. The Church Treasurer should be able to record all church receipts, manage trust funds and local church offerings, record expenses, generate reports, print statements, submit conference remittances, and edit records before they are finalized. 

Upon login, users should be welcomed with a modern dashboard displaying important financial information in real time. The dashboard should include cards showing today's total collections, monthly income, trust funds, local church funds, available balance, pending remittances, cash at hand, cash at bank, and recent transactions. It should also include charts showing weekly and monthly collection trends, offering distributions, and cash flow analysis. Quick action buttons should allow users to create a new receipt, enter a cash statement, record expenses, generate reports, or print documents directly from the dashboard.

The system should include a Church Profile module where administrators can store information such as church name, district, conference, church logo, address, financial year, pastor's name, treasurer's name, elder's name, and contact information. This information should automatically appear on all printed reports and financial statements.

A comprehensive Receipt Management module should automatically generate sequential receipt numbers and record every financial transaction. Each receipt should capture the receipt number, transaction date, member name, membership number, phone number, department, payment method (cash, M-Pesa, bank transfer, or cheque), amount received, offering category, description, remarks, and the user who recorded the transaction. Every receipt should be printable and searchable.

The Cash Statement module should closely resemble the official SDA Church Treasurer's Cash Statement form. It should allow the treasurer to enter receipt numbers, receipt totals, tithe, combined offering, camp meeting offering, building fund, church offerings, trust funds, local church funds, special offerings, and any other income. It should also record payment methods including cash, cheque, bank deposits, and M-Pesa. The system should automatically calculate subtotals, grand totals, balances, and generate a printable statement identical to the official paper form.

The Trust Funds module should manage all conference-related offerings, including tithe(100 % to conference), combined offering(50 % to conference  and 50% to local), camp meeting offerings(100% to conference), mission offerings, Sabbath School offerings, evangelism funds, station development, field funds, communication, education, stewardship, youth ministries, women's ministries, children's ministries, health ministries, family life, publishing, community services, thanksgiving (50 % to conference  and 50% to local,),firstfruit(50 % to conference  and 50% to local,)and any additional trust fund categories created by the administrator.others 100 % goes to local fund. The system should automatically separate trust funds from local church funds and generate summaries for conference remittance.

The Local Church Funds module should record offerings and donations that remain within the local church. Categories should include church offerings, building fund, development fund, choir fund, youth fund, Pathfinder fund, Adventurer fund, Dorcas fund, maintenance, water, electricity, pastor welfare, camp meeting expenses, special projects, and any other custom categories. Administrators should be able to add, edit, or remove categories as needed.

The Weekly Cash Count and Offering Report Summary should mirror the official SDA report by organizing collections into Week One through Week Five. The system should automatically calculate weekly totals, monthly totals, trust fund summaries, local church fund summaries, cash received, bank deposits, M-Pesa collections, and overall balances without requiring manual calculations.

The Monthly Cash Statement should automatically generate complete financial summaries showing opening balances, total receipts, trust funds collected, local church income, total expenses, transfers, conference remittances, closing balances, cash at hand, cash at bank, and outstanding balances. The report should be printable in a professional layout matching the official SDA church format.

A Conference Remittance module should simplify the process of submitting trust funds to the conference by recording cash remitted, bank deposits, M-Pesa transactions, cheque payments, transaction reference numbers, deposit slips, bank names, remittance dates, and total amounts. The module should automatically prepare a printable remittance report.

The Expense Management module should enable treasurers to record all church expenditures. Each expense should include an expense number, transaction date, expense category, description, vendor or payee, amount, payment method, approval information, supporting documents, and remarks. Expenses should automatically reduce the available church balance and appear in monthly financial reports.

The system should also include a Member Management module where member details such as membership number, full name, phone number, department, family information, membership status, and contribution history are stored. Treasurers should be able to search members quickly when recording offerings.

A comprehensive Reporting module should generate daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, and annual financial reports. Reports should include trust fund summaries, local church fund summaries, expense reports, cash flow statements, offering analyses, collection trends, income statements, conference remittance summaries, and top contributor reports. Every report should be exportable to PDF, Excel, and CSV formats while maintaining professional formatting.

Powerful search and filtering capabilities should allow users to search transactions by member name, receipt number, phone number, amount, date, payment method, offering category, or fund type. This will enable quick retrieval of historical financial records.

The system should include notifications and reminders for pending monthly reports, overdue conference remittances, unusually high cash balances, missing receipts, and scheduled backups. These notifications should help treasurers maintain compliance with church financial procedures.

An Audit Trail should automatically record every user activity including login, logout, creation, editing, deletion, printing, exporting, and report generation. Each audit record should include the user, date, time, and activity description to improve accountability and simplify financial audits.

The system should support automatic daily backups, manual backups, and cloud storage. Administrators should be able to restore previous backups whenever necessary to prevent data loss.

Security should be a priority throughout the application. The system should use secure authentication, encrypted passwords, role-based access control, row-level security, automatic session timeouts, and comprehensive activity logging to protect sensitive financial information.

All financial reports should be professionally designed and printable in A4 landscape format, matching the official Seventh-day Adventist Church Treasurer forms. Printed documents should include the church logo, church information, signature sections for the treasurer, pastor, elder, and auditor, and maintain a clean appearance suitable for official church records.

The application should also include interactive financial charts and analytics showing monthly income trends, weekly offering comparisons, trust fund distributions, local church fund distributions, annual cash flow, and historical financial performance. These visualizations should help church leaders understand financial trends and make informed decisions.

The system should use a modern technology stack consisting of React.js or Next.js for the frontend, Tailwind CSS and TypeScript for responsive design, Supabase with PostgreSQL for the backend database, secure authentication with Row Level Security, and Vercel for deployment. Additional features should include dark mode, offline support through Progressive Web App (PWA) technology, automatic saving of forms, Excel import and export, PDF generation, barcode or QR code receipt numbers, church logo uploads, email and SMS notifications, multi-church support, financial year locking, opening balance management, automatic closing balance calculations, bank reconciliation, M-Pesa transaction imports, receipt reprinting, duplicate transaction detection, custom report generation, and a financial analytics dashboard.

The completed application should fully digitize the SDA Church Treasurer's Cash Statement, Weekly Offerings Report Summary, and Conference Remittance process, eliminating manual calculations while maintaining compliance with official SDA accounting procedures.
